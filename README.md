A broadsheet-style front page summing up Friday 27 November 2026, drawn programmatically in pure Python. The hero story is the Thanksgiving storm that left nearly a million homes without power across the US East Coast, with 400+ flights cancelled and the Macy's parade postponed for the first time in its 100-year history. Also covered: the drone attack on a vital military site in Kuwait that killed two Italian technicians (Kuwait blames Iran; Iran denies any role), Zelenskyy warning of a new mass Russian strike while Trump says he pressed Putin to pull back, and the cyberattack that took news agency AFP's client platform offline. An In Brief strip adds the EU split over a 45bn euro loan for Ukraine, overnight missile strikes on Kyiv, and the US envoy's word that Putin agreed not to use nuclear arms.
